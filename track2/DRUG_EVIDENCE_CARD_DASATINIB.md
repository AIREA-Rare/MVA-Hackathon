- **Exact rescue hypothesis and competing explanations:** dasatinib lowers the senescent-cell burden in BUBR1-insufficient cells without increasing missegregation. Competing explanations: no effect on missegregation; removing senescent cells could promote cancer.
- **Target expression, relevant compartment, exposure evidence and model concentration range:** no exposure or concentration data in a BUBR1 model. In-vitro senolytic concentrations come from published cell work (abstract level).

- **Interaction, formulation or combination rationale:** single agent. The quercetin combination is out of scope.
- **Contradictions; why similar candidates were rejected:** senolysis depends on cell type. K5 category: plausible but indirect. Acetylcysteine and other antioxidants were rejected because of tumour-promotion evidence.
- **Proximal, chromosome-segregation and viability readouts:** senescent fraction; segregation errors per division in the surviving cells; viability of non-senescent cells.
