# Track 2 detailed report — DRAFT TEMPLATE

**Evidence status:** literature/computational hypothesis unless actual experiments are linked. **Patient benefit:** unmeasured. **PatchLens integration:** deferred by default.

1. Summary: disease mechanism, approved-drug hypothesis or conditional branches, strongest evidence and decisive unresolved issue.
2. Team, GitHub URL, contribution/tool/LLM disclosures and data/consent boundaries. Include an abstract up to 500 words and the relevant organizer form answers in the organizer's methods form.
3. Case evidence allowed for release: phenotype, candidate pair, phase/technical uncertainty, causal passport.
4. Scientific rationale: gene → transcript/protein/complex → pathway → direction; primary citations and contradictions.
5. Repurposing eligibility: marketed approval/label, pharmacology, exposure and combination rationale if used.
6. Candidate selection and rejection: public/proprietary sources, automated versus manual discovery, expert curation, applicability, competing drugs and reasons.
7. Evidence: separate literature, synthetic demo, patient computations and real measured experiments. Optional accepted structural E4 evidence with `call: null`.
8. Validation: model, controls, endpoints, replication, uncertainty, target engagement, CIN and toxicity stopping rules.
9. Potential impact and transfer limits: relevant next study, no patient dosing/efficacy claim.
10. Reproducibility/scalability: environments, references, commands, passports, synthetic public fixtures and resource needs; actual analysis effort or clearly labeled estimates.
11. Limitations and next falsification test; missing evidence and conditional mechanism alternatives.
12. Privacy, licensing, embargo/deletion/retention boundaries; references and public video URL.

## Figure plan

One source-backed causal evidence diagram, one candidate/rejection table, and one validation design panel. Proposed results stay labeled proposed; no simulated curve stands in for measured rescue. Every performance claim needs cohort/n and comparator.
